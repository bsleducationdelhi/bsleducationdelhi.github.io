For employers

What this check tells you

Every certificate this institute issues is entered in a register held by the office. A result on this page is read live from that register, so it reflects the current position, not the day the certificate was printed. If a certificate has been withdrawn, the page says so plainly.

Nothing found? The certificate may predate the online register, or a character may have been mistyped. The office can confirm any record by phone.
